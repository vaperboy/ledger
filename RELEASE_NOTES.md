# What's new
- Joint ventures are safer and clearer: a company with partners is sold only by a board vote (and not under 80% of its value), partners cannot leave in the lock-up and can be bought out after it, board meetings remind you to vote, and a capital call no longer creates money; startups cost more to open the bigger the plan, a season warns before unclaimed rewards are lost, and a big move in the electricity price goes in the news
- One global electricity price that follows how much power companies use and how much plants supply, a World prices card in the Market app, and bank, insurance, fund, casino and football club can no longer be started
- A preview Store with gold (cosmetics, boxes, slots; never in-game money), boxes with published odds and a guarantee, a Cabinet of artifacts, and live auctions paid in gold
- Joint ventures: co-own a company with friends at a price you set for each, with planned and urgent board meetings, voting by share, and decisions the game applies
- Startups funded by other players (Ventures, Startup market): browse by trade, stage and viral, open a startup for a high entry cost, invest in three escrow-released stages at a price set by a fixed rule, with caps, a resale lock-up, a price band and taxes; every action first says what will happen; the other players are a local stand-in for now
- Rivals with named competitors and a story of their moves, a weekly plan decision, and the Desk: one small question a day with a notebook that fills over time
- Popups are rounded at the bottom as well, the top of the screen shows the photo instead of black, and Group and Ventures are locked with a lock and an unlock popup until you earn them; Pick tab only offers tabs you have unlocked
- A guided first game: the tutorial tells you what to press, you pick your first product and price before anything else, the Inbox points to what to read, and Your next goal opens a list of the goals being tracked
- No more auto-hire or buy-everything: a new company opens with the essentials, and a chain store can appoint a manager
- Suppliers show what each business really buys, grouped by what is supplied, with a choice of suppliers that changes cost, quality and reliability, and a Supply other players screen
- The free Pro pass can no longer be repeated by starting a new game, Plus keeps working during Apple's grace period, purchases are remembered offline, and the paywall lists only what exists
- Free players can double a week's reward or take a second chance after a bankruptcy, and ads are capped per day
- A Season Pass in the Phone tab (30 tiers, free and Plus tracks, cosmetic and comfort rewards only)
- A quarterly review of your group with targets and grades, and an annual report with a shareholder letter you can share
- A first-open account screen (Apple, Google or play as a guest), an Account section in Settings (link, sign out, export and delete your data), profiles with privacy levels, and a Friends app on the Phone with requests, search by name or code and friends' companies; the online side is a local stand-in for now and the game works fully offline
- A blurred photo backdrop with Apple Liquid Glass on the Home boxes, main navigation and popups (on iOS 26 and later; earlier versions get a drawn glass look)
- Fix for the crash on opening a game that has a report waiting: the report popup no longer overflows the screen stack
- Fix for a crash on opening an older game when the Custom tab pointed at a company that no longer exists
- Every newly opened tab, company page and key phone app now gets its own short guided tour, shown once
- Each company starts by choosing one of three products, the rest come later under add product, Sandbox mode needs Ledger Pro, and the New range option is gone
- Equipment opens straight into one structured list, showing machines, vehicles and software only where the business uses them
- Home sits clear of the island, Next day sits above the tab bar, empty space is shared evenly, a single company shows a richer card, and the app has a soft gradient background with glass-style cards, tab bar and day button
- Fix for a crash while playing the first weeks of a new game: more number conversions can no longer overflow or hit a non-number, and charts ignore values that are not numbers
- Fix for the crash on opening an older save: saved numbers can no longer overflow a conversion, duplicate names no longer break lookups, the first-run tour only starts in a new game, and the zoom transitions are off until they are proven safe
- Money in is a green up arrow and money out a red down arrow instead of plus and minus; a company shows this week so far live with its change on last week; only the change in a figure rolls (up in green, down in red); the net worth chart adds each new day instead of redrawing
- A holding company sells nothing and no longer earns interest on idle cash; its income is dividends from your companies and gains on money it invests
- Events can chain (an ignored breach becomes a claim, then a press story; late invoices, a skipped inspection and price wars escalate), each of the twelve starter trades has its own events, big owners get a fourth answer, daily goals vary with streak perks, a recap shows when you return, and Stats has a "How you are playing" panel
- Choose Classic, Sandbox or Hard when you start; rivals bid for the best accounts and staff of a top company, markets warn before a crash, big groups lose creditor confidence, and a bad event your team handled shows as "Survived it"
- Better feel: every button press and choice has a haptic, milestones and trophies are celebrated full-screen, a sounds option (off by default), a game-over screen with a one-tap retry, a four-step first-session tour, and a short help sheet the first time you open each phone app
- Companies and Phone apps open with a zoom, payroll, loans and large sales send a small chip to your net worth, charts draw in from the left, a cash shortfall opens the loan application with the amount filled in, and two-answer decisions are answered on the Home card
- The game runs day by day: no work is saved up for the end of the week, each company and process runs on its own day, and month-end money (tax, VAT, loan instalments) lands on the real last day of the month
- Things that cannot be measured are described in words instead of numbers (worker performance, name recognition, equipment condition, risk, standing); money, wages, ownership and tax stay as numbers
- Next day is a floating button above the tab bar and Skip week is gone, the daily report popup is back, and Home never scrolls with one company
- Unlocks belong to the player: once one company earns a feature it is open for every company, new ones start with it, and the unlock popup shows once
- The manager screen has five orders (Direction, Budget, Headcount, Pay and bonus, Replace) with a staffing plan, a weekly digest and read-only targets with one yearly budget approval
- Boards are simpler: one decision list (sell, close, dividend, CEO, capital, open or close a location) with a recommendation per item, one quarterly review with the CEO (Direction and Budget), and chain standards that every location follows
- Taxes are paid as they arise: dividend tax when a dividend is paid, gains tax at the sale, interest and wealth tax monthly, company tax monthly, and the year end only settles the small difference
- No banners or toasts at the top: a decision is answered in a popup and a result popup follows with what you chose and what happened; plain information lives in Stats, Inbox and the ledger pages
- Managers act realistically: they move prices within a band set by your Direction, order stock and run marketing within their budget, and each department head owns one decision; you give five orders (Direction, Budget, Headcount, Pay and bonus, Replace)
- Hiring starts simple (offer the going rate, below or above) and the perks, promises and expenses unlock with five staff or a manager; settings show sole trader and limited company side by side at your current profit
- The daily report is a one-line bar on Home instead of a blocking popup, and Stats has one ledger that answers why money moved, with search and filters, plus Goals and Reports as pages
- Pricing: each trade has its own price sensitivity with a market reference that follows quality and rivals; the price popup shows how far you are from the market and a one-line forecast
- Chain page leads with a head office and location cards (manager, health, one action), holding stakes are grouped by size with one clear button each, and every company shows who it belongs to and who runs it
- Managers have one budget (tight, normal or free) instead of several limits; tax screens explain the yearly tax and a pay-yourself sheet compares salary, dividend and keeping profit in the company
- Goals: one ladder with no cash gifts (rewards raise credit, awareness or reputation), a streak-only daily goal, and the ambition choice is a Home card after the first profitable month; unlocks follow one rule table and insurance and suppliers open earlier
- Group has a Structure tab: a tree of what you own and who runs it, with ownership, manager, health and one action per entry; Boardroom says whose board it is; tabs renamed (People, Grow, Owners and sales)
- Personal tax: a monthly estimate with an early warning, paid from cash then savings with each tax shown separately, a credit line that grows with wealth, and payment options (sell investments, loan, 12-month plan) before any forced sale
- Hiring is one staffing plan: a target headcount per role, filled by the responsible manager after a two-week search with a recruiting fee, seasonal crews included
- Each company gets its own first-year demand boost, you are told in the Inbox when the early protections end, and bad events now give a real second choice
- Chains: a chain has its own head-office page, new locations are named after districts (never numbers), adding a location is one step, and a chain manager fills every location manager automatically
- Boards: a branch shares its parent company board, managers are no longer seated as directors, and directors decide the CEO when an outside owner sits on the board
- Fixes: a holding loan always covers the full price, and a fund commitment at exactly the shown minimum is accepted
- Interface polish: cards have a soft edge and top highlight, sliders have a larger thumb with a ring and only snap when close to a stop, popup corners are tighter, corner radii are slightly rounder, and screens that do not fit now scroll instead of cropping
- Ventures quotes come from the engine; set your own asking price when selling a stake (counter-offers and declines are shown); holdings can buy stakes in other founders' startups; Group stakes can be bought
- Holdings can borrow to buy a stake, turn a fully owned company into a division, sell shares you moved in, and hold board talks; a single chosen first product no longer sells like a full range
- Ventures is one portfolio page inside Projects: your startups, backing others, funds, group stakes and software, with a cost summary before founding and buy/sell popups; the Venture tile opens it in place
- Holding companies can buy full or partial stakes in your companies and in rivals (passive, influence, control, squeeze-out), take board seats, sell and merge stakes
- Home: net worth counts up after a report closes and shows your personal account with last week and last month; "All" opens every company in a popup; more companies fit on screen; the yearly tax note itemises each charge
- Choose what a new company sells first (only chosen products sell); price limit now depends on the trade; each screen shows only its own feature with no jump links; payroll approvals are answered in the Inbox; Group, Company, Market and Reports redirects removed; Market app, Home skip week, Group tabs and Company marketing steps added
- One broken machine no longer stops a business: it cuts only what depends on it (a café without its display fridge stops chilled items, about a third fewer customers), and the weekly note says what was lost
- Sliders move only on a sideways drag, so scrolling never changes a value by accident
- Group redesigned: group value and trend, what needs you, Boardroom where you can talk to every board member, sale routes, chain page with branch close/sell
- No more jumping between tabs: links open inside the tab you are on with Back, a company switcher on company and group pages; outside apps redesigned (TechPro, AirStay, Atlas, Marketplace, Luxury, Reviews, Market, Ventures) with a readable status bar
- Redesigned Home (one screen: net worth, what needs you or your next goal, top companies, Next day), Team (people and cost, one worker profile, offer page that shows what you know), Phone home, Bank and Accounting
- A business that served no one last week now says why (e.g. a required machine in repair) and opens the fix
- Popups on light screens (Bank, Accounting, outside apps) are readable: light glass with dark text; buttons are capsules everywhere
- Chains work like one company: one board, one dividend from the whole chain, branches share cash; a losing branch is closed (lease, staff moved or paid off, stock moved) or sold as a going concern, never bankrupt on its own
- Popups rebuilt: three sizes (compact, medium, detail) that fit their content exactly, centred glass cards with an event colour, press-and-hold for irreversible actions
- Speed: screens redraw much faster (money text, Home, Group, Phone badges and Accounting lists no longer redo whole-game work on every redraw); text on filled buttons, chips and photo banners always uses the fill's readable ink
- Polish: one supplier list across Accounting, Group and Chain; calmer Ventures tab header; new-player trade picker matches the in-game table; popup and toast type on the shared scale
- Screens redesigned: Home scrolls (net worth, Needs you, companies), answers in two taps with a toast; company Overview, Manage, Sales, item pages, suppliers, equipment and setup decluttered; one worker profile, one applicants list, hiring without the extra toggle; Group pages, Treasury, sale routes and Boardroom calmer; new-company flow reviews the cost before paying; Bank flatter, payroll lives in Accounting
- Look: new design system: one type scale that follows your text size, one spacing and corner set, unboxed sections and rows, three button styles with full-size tap areas, buttons say why they are disabled, no gradients or glows
- Fixes from play-testing: volume-discount and franchise buyout sizing early in the game year, desk notices always name who handles them, no flood after replacing a CEO, pay requests capped per week
- Ownership: Boardroom and Ownership tabs in Group; owner meetings (one-to-one, board round, strategy), CEO replacement by vote, advisory board for sole owners; sale routes: handshake, broker, auction, buyout, merger, stake tiers, sell to a group, master franchise; old sell buttons elsewhere removed
- Notices: one payroll notice a week handled by your finance desk; routine items no longer fill Needs you; share buying removed from Invest/Bank (sell what you hold)
- Gift codes: Settings > Redeem a code opens the full game for a year (or longer) on this device
- Popups: action buttons stay pinned under long sheets; one signing popup, one info button, one text input; no cards inside popup cards; typed values survive a backdrop tap
- Notices: minor "cannot / not enough" results show as a short toast instead of a blocking popup; destructive imports and sales ask first; red alert and banners follow the app palette

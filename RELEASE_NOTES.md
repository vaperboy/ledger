# What's new
- Reports and research arrive as proper documents in your inbox (letterhead, sections, figures) and in Stats; brand is shown in words (Unknown, Emerging, Known locally, Established, Respected, Trusted, Iconic), never as a number; a manager needs approval from the level above to hire an assistant manager
- Phone: every app tile and the Phone tab show a red count of what is new since you last opened the app (Inbox, Bank, Accounting, Market, News, Weather, Stats, AirStay, TechPro, Atlas), and new updates arrive as one banner per app (at most two a day) that opens the app
- Chain and franchise now need what real life needs (a proven profitable year, a registered trademark, an operations manual, a legal disclosure document and support staff); equipment under warranty is repaired for free without asking; a manager needs approval from the role above to hire an assistant manager; market research arrives as a proper document in your inbox
- Home has no "All clear" card; the daily report is a short morning briefing with an insight and an action; popups highlight one recommended option; report numbers count up; TechPro pages never show up blank
- Group: Money is one screen with cash, loans and dividends, dividend policy has its own popup, the board shows every seat with who can fill it, Management picks the company inside a chain, and the Ventures link and Who decides what are gone
- Awards reflect results, not setup: opening a company, hiring or signing documents no longer fire awards; awards now come from profitable months, a manager running a profitable company, three profitable months in a row, being debt free and group reputation
- Main tabs are Ventures, Group, Home, your own tab, Phone (long-press the custom tab to choose what it opens); the Team tab is gone; switching tabs is by tapping only and slides the page; the black strip at the top of the screen is filled
- Fix: popups are exactly as high as their content (before, many stretched to 80% of the screen with empty space above the text); decision and report popups too
- Pictures for vehicles, premises, equipment, software and insurance now show on TechPro, My items, AirStay and Atlas; neighbouring listings use different pictures
- Team is merged into Group: each company page has a Management tab (you, managers, executives, Who decides what, open posts); Dividends are inside Money; Add to group opens New company, Holding, Join a franchise and Football club; Expand abroad is per company; a company's Team tab shows staff only
- Phone: the Market app is organised in five parts; Goals and Reports are no longer apps (Goals and awards and Previous reports are in Stats); Ventures and Projects are one Ventures page; the weather app no longer links to your companies; rewards and achievements must be claimed, and an achievement popup shows its art and title
- Group works like Banking: Group opens a company and chain picker with value arrows and percentages; each entity has its own overview, Board (thin seat rows), Dividends, Expansion (chain and franchise, moved out of company management), Money and Deals; a group-wide supplier directory lists every supplier with a profile; Structure, More and the group office are gone and Giving moved to Bank
- New systems show up inside existing screens: a growth path and Promote in the worker popup, brand value on Group and Sales, market research in Sales and Market, thank-you buttons and consequence lines in market news; realistic product and service options for every business type; market news is rarer and states what it costs you; competitors react when you undercut them; managers propose bigger premises; only managers hire
- Summary screens (Team, Group, Projects, Phone home, Goals, Bank account) fit one screen and do not scroll; long lists scroll only vertically; the screen behind a popup is locked
- Switching main tabs slides sideways like a pager, a tab-bar tap always opens that tab's root, taps cannot pass through during a swipe, and each tab keeps its own place when you swipe between them
- Suppliers are grouped and compact with a details popup and one payment-terms control; TechPro has a chip for every real item family and more sort options; report figures count up
- Fix: popups no longer show up empty and are compact; Perks opens as a popup right on Team; no more notification for payments received; Marketing is one compact list; slimmer sliders and switches everywhere; tab rows no longer touch
- Popups size to their content; recognition banners open the right worker; a rival group entering a country where you trade shows in Market
- Accessibility: steppers, confirm boxes and Team rows have spoken labels and hints, and small one-line labels shrink instead of cutting off
- Market news (big contracts, companies for sale, competitor and supplier moves, recognition) arrives as banners and highlighted rows in Market; the Payment banner follows the largest real payment of the day; Sales has a market trend card; the bank flows no longer rise from the bottom; old unused screens are gone
- Talking terms are two clean one-page steps: contract terms, then pay (pay basis, commission, benefits and expectations are back)
- Notifications look like iOS banners: they slide down from the top, swipe up to dismiss, urgent ones such as payroll stay until you act, and tapping opens the decision or asks if you want to be taken there; the Recent list is gone from Home
- Home is one locked screen that fits everything: companies get the space first (the full list is in Group), Recent only uses what is left; net worth and the four figures count up only after you press Continue on the report
- Adding a product or service opens a popup with choices named for your company, their development time and cost; profit and market change unlock once you hire a market advisor, a new role
- Popups open in the middle of the screen in the report style instead of rising from the bottom; Marketing is a compact popup that fits without scrolling; the new menu item row is gone from Sales
- Animations: team hires, promotions, manager and board seats, group connections and acquisitions, new stores, project progress, market charts, loan debt, goals, luxury purchases, a Home events timeline and a cleaner milestone celebration
- Animations: figures count up, profit glows green, big payments slide in, loans visibly shrink, charts draw themselves, goals fill and unlock in gold, luxury purchases get a gold card, milestones use a clean gold ring, Home lists recent important events
- Team: the worker list is at the top with a Hire workers button (the owner and manager always show, with free workplaces counted); tap a worker for a popup with salary, contract, promises, mood and hours; hiring is one Hire page (role, job ad or agency) and one terms page; Who decides what opens real options; Management, Hire someone, Promises and Network tabs are gone; AirStay shows office places
- Hiring: offices fit a limited number of people, workers remind you of promises before they are due, job ads cost money and applicants arrive over time, agencies cost more and take a share of pay for two years, extra roles such as a security guard have real effects
- Bank: the balance card and its buttons are only on the Account tab; screens no longer wiggle sideways in Group, Projects and Bank; full-page popups fade and scale in
- Sales lists your own products and services (named for your company): tap one for a price slider with live, unrounded numbers, per-item discounts and how it is sold; lower prices raise demand; add a new product or service; Marketing is a popup; Pricing and How you sell are gone. Items can be listed for your other companies in TechPro, with the profit going to the selling company
- Net-worth milestones (100k, 250k, 500k, 1M and on) and profit-streak awards (4, 8, 13, 26, 52 weeks) pop up as celebrations
- TechPro store is a grid of product groups; opening one shows its suppliers as boxes, and every product has its own page
- Money figures count up and flash green when they rise and red when they fall (home, group, reports, bank, team, company cash); switching tabs slides and fades instead of jumping
- Swiping between tabs needs a longer, clearly sideways drag, so it no longer triggers by accident
- Swipe sideways to change the tab in your Inbox and in a bank account
- New game: you can pick an ambition at the start, or decide later; chains and franchises now need real things (profit, cash, reputation, open stores, documents) instead of waiting years; a settled lawsuit can no longer leave wages unpaid
- Group: six plain tabs (Overview, Structure, Board, Money, Deals, More); dividends and giving are on Money, and More is one flat page with the group office, posts, stand-alone companies and the market, no menu to pick from
- Chain registration, franchise documents and franchise brand applications are signed on a paper form first (tick the terms, draw your signature), then reviewed
- Starting a company fills in nothing: you sign each document yourself on the Setup screen and choose the place and equipment yourself. The old setup page is gone
- Atlas Insurance takes every policy: no insurer has to be chosen first, and the insurer, advisers and security tab is removed from Atlas
- A manager (or a higher rank) now takes the decisions that are theirs: small matters against your net worth are answered for you, and Inbox has Needs you, Handled, Risks and All; the daily report lists what was handled
- Team: Overview and a new Authority tab no longer repeat each other; Ventures lives in the Phone app and is linked from Group > Deals; Projects has four tabs
- Shareholder dividend policies now send a quarterly proposal you answer instead of paying by themselves; Inbox levels follow the kind and size of a decision against your net worth
- Pages that fit the screen no longer wobble sideways, and rows of chips that fit stay still
- Documents and licences are filled in on a paper form: tick the terms, draw your signature, send. The authority answers after its review time and can refuse; you can apply again at once. VAT registration is always on the Setup list
- Manage > Licences: apply for the licences that new things to sell need
- A company being set up has one fixed Setup screen: what is missing plus TechPro Store and AirStay buttons in their own colours; no stats until it opens
- Daily report every day, even a quiet one: shows how your cash and net worth moved. Only Continue closes it
- A company about to be force-sold now shows a see-through red alert first
- Answered decisions show a result card: what you chose, which company, and what changed
- Loans: banks also lend against what a business is worth, not only its profit
- Settings: "How much I decide" removed. Payroll, replacing leavers and complaints follow who you hired; managers' spending limit grows with your net worth
- Group > Dividends: dividends are never automatic; pay a share or let a holding collect
- Team > Delegation: shows who handles payroll, hiring and complaints in each company; Inbox decisions show a level
- TechPro: every item for sale to any company; tabs Store, Products, My items (with what is missing) and My orders
- Starting a company buys and hires nothing; the start-up screen shows a recommended investment and takes only what is needed
- Pricing: type the price per unit or hour; shops set a markup percent
- Owner hours can go under 40; subscriptions have no sales (only a discount on the biggest plan); your own delivery company charges a fee

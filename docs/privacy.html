Ledger Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Ledger is a company management simulation game. This policy explains what data the game uses.

Who we are

Ledger is published by Matas Dovyda. Contact: matas1114@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Anonymous account. The game creates an anonymous account so your progress, profile and online features work. No name, email or phone number is required.
  • Game data. Your save, company results, rankings, auction and friend activity, display name and chosen profile look are stored on our servers to run the online world.
  • Purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive confirmation of what you bought, never your payment details.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, we store a device token to send game notifications. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.
  • Advertising. If you choose to watch optional rewarded ads, the ad provider may process an advertising identifier. This only happens if you allow tracking when asked.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not read your contacts, photos or location.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase). Your display name, rank and profile look are visible to other players. Everything else is private to you.

Your choices

You can delete your account and data by contacting us at the address above, and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

Ledger is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

# New look, approved 27 Oct 2026

The owner approved these mockups (made-up figures, looks only) as the way the whole app must look. Open the `.dc.html` files in a browser to see them.

- `HomeMix` Home (the tab formerly called Company): title and Hello at left, date and New company at right.
- `PhoneHome` the Phone tab: an app grid, notification bubbles on app icons and a banner.
- `BankAccounts` and `BankAccount` the Bank app: pick an account, then manage it. Loans live inside their account.
- `PickA` choosing a trade: sharp table, columns Start-up, Demand, Staff (no margin).
- `CreateA` setting up the company after choosing the trade.
- `Hiring` (option A) and `Negotiation`.

Rules that came with them: cards and icons are the house style; only Next Day is a filled button; the title and "Hello, name" appear only on Home; a small outlined "New company" button replaces the plus; Inbox is an app inside Phone, not a tab; when no manager or accountant approves payroll, the Bank app icon and that company's account row carry a red bubble; every screen outside Home has a round-cornered back button and its own title.

## Group, Team and Projects (approved, each file is a TAB of its screen)

- Group: `GroupA` Overview, `GroupB` Structure, `GroupC` Board. Group has no Finances tab: money lives in the Bank app. Remaining tabs (Deals, More) follow the same pattern.
- Team: `TeamA` Overview, `TeamB` People, `TeamC` Delegation. Vacancies follows the same pattern.
- Projects: `ProjectsA` Running, `ProjectsB` Timeline, `ProjectsC` Bids and offers. Finished and Ventures follow the same pattern.

## 2 Nov 2026, hiring rebuild (engine side)
- Workplaces: an office holds floor(m2 / 9) people, at least 2; working from home holds 3; shops, workshops, warehouses, clinics and every other premises are unlimited. The owner counts as one while there is no manager and his hours are above zero (`PremisesExtras.heads`). Hiring beyond capacity is refused (`hire` returns refused; `hireOffer` returns termsRefused with the reason; `hireBlock(companyID:)` reads it). Automatic hirers are not capped.
- Promises per worker: read with `promises(of:companyID:)`. Two weeks before the date the worker brings it up in the inbox ("Keep it now" / "Ask to wait"), once per date. With a manager the question is level 2 (manager) in `Delegation.level` and the manager keeps it; without one it waits for the owner. Waiting moves the date by 3 weeks, at most twice; each wait ends any cheer; the second wait leaves the worker out of patience: the `denied` mark (mood line, more quit risk) and a 30 percent chance (deterministic per promise) that they hand in notice. After that the option becomes "Let it slide" (promise broken now). Keeping: salary review gives 5 percent, promotion or more responsibility 4 percent, training sends them on the first course; other kinds are marked kept.
- Job ads: fee = scaledPrice(800) x (1 + 5 percent per head up to 40) x role pay against the helper wage (0.6 to 3), booked as an expense. Needs the company name and organisation number (registration stores none: the number is derived from the seed and company id, "55xxxx-xxxx"). The ad runs 28 days; applicants arrive on random days within it (count: 2 + 0.5 per head + reputation/25 + 1.2 x log10 of weekly revenue, -1..+1), each with an info message "<name> applied for <role>" (handled by the manager's name when there is one). Applicants stay on the list for 8 weeks. One running ad per role.
- Agency: replaces the player's network option (`SearchMode.network` is deprecated, kept for old saves and automatic hirers; not in `hiringSearchOptions`). Fee = 8 x the ad fee. 3 suggestions at once (+6 skill over a plain candidate), then one better one a week for 3 weeks (+12, +14, +16). The agency keeps 8 percent of the hired person's monthly wage for 24 months, booked at each month end as "Agency fee".
- Role catalog (extras by trade family) and effects, when filled by a hire of that role or by a duty (`setDuty`): security guard: theft/shrink losses x0.70, staff-theft growth x0.5; marketing responsible: +8 percent campaign awareness gain (+18 percent when the owner works under 20 hours there); receptionist: +4 percent awareness; developer: development projects take 75 percent of the weeks (rounded up); accountant: inspection fault chance -5 points; cleaner: -4 points.
- Worker profile: performance = skill with what they have learned; expected = 55 x wage / going pay (20 to 90); met when performance is within 5 of it.
- CI: engine-tests and app-build cancel an unfinished older run on the same branch when a new push arrives (the Mac queue had grown to 34 runs of about 45 minutes each); release keeps its own queue. Owner approved.
- New product and service options (`newItemOptions`, `addNewItem`): 4-5 company-named choices, none already sold. A variant of what the trade sells (same goods/service kind) is added at once, free. A new category (service for a goods trade, goods for a service trade) is a Development project "newitem": 6 weeks + 2 x sqrt(size), at most 20 (developer role x0.75), cost 2 percent of a year sales (min scaledPrice(40000)) paid at start, one project at a time; on completion `SalesItems.add` runs (no luck roll). Profit/market numbers are estimates (variant lift 2 percent of units, category 5 percent, divided by 1 + 0.25 per rival; profit = extra units x ticket x (1 - cost of sales); market = change in own size / (own + rivals) share) and are nil unless a market advisor is employed or holds the duty. Market advisor: role "advisor" (wage 62000 base), recommended for every family except hold, fin, energy.
- Market events log (`MarketEvents`, `rest["mev"]`; reads `marketEvents(limit:)` newest first, `largestPaymentToday(companyID:)`, `marketTrend(companyID:)`): informational only, no inbox messages, no random draws, no money. Cap 60, oldest dropped. Hooks: competitorLaunch = a rival's discount offer or a new store next to you (`Rivals.behave`); majorContract = a volume-contract offer worth >= 5 percent of yearly sales (`VolumeContracts.offers`, skipped when yearly sales are 0) or a key-account offer (`KeyAccounts.offer`); recognition = a kept promise, a finished course (`StaffProfile.takeCourse`, `GroupPeople.train`) or a hire anniversary. Largest payment: biggest single booking per company and day from daily takings ("Customers") and weekly invoice settlement ("Invoiced customers"); day key week*7+day so it resets by itself. Market trend: six weekly samples of the market price index read as 30 daily points by linear interpolation; change = today against 28 days ago.

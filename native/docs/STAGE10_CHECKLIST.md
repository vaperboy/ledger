# Stage 10 device checklist (owner)

All items: needs device. Format: do this, expect that. Note anything that differs.

## Home
- [ ] Press Continue on the report: net worth and the four figures count up only after Continue.
- [ ] Look at Home with 1, 5 and 12 companies: nothing scrolls, companies get space first, Recent uses what is left.
- [ ] Hold the chart: the held value shows and releases cleanly.

## Team and hiring
- [ ] Company, Team, Hire workers: a pushed page, not a sheet from the bottom.
- [ ] Open a candidate: step 1 contract terms, step 2 pay (basis, commission, benefits, expectations); Back returns one step.
- [ ] Add and remove a promise; Offer less / Offer more move the offer.
- [ ] Management rows open their post; VoiceOver reads the row and says "Opens this post".
- [ ] Goals and Perks rows each open a full page; Back returns to Team.

## Sales, pricing, add item
- [ ] Company, Sales: the market trend card shows and updates after a week.
- [ ] Add item: locked items show a lock and the reason; an unlocked item can be added.
- [ ] Change a price: the result shows in Sales after the week.

## TechPro
- [ ] Open TechPro Store, buy equipment: it appears under Manage, Equipment.
- [ ] Product photos render.

## Group
- [ ] Group lists every company and sorts by Needs attention, Profit, Revenue, Cash, Name.
- [ ] With many companies the search and chain filter work; the clear (x) button clears the search.
- [ ] Tap a company: it opens; Back returns to Group.

## Projects
- [ ] A project company shows Projects under Sales; start a project and see the timeline steps (done / current / next).
- [ ] Open and close tenders; progress bars animate (not under Reduce Motion).

## Bank
- [ ] Bank, Loans, apply: steps are pushes (nothing rises from the bottom); Back goes one step.
- [ ] Bills tab: the rules section opens and closes.

## Inbox, banners, popups
- [ ] Market news (big contract, company for sale, competitor move) arrives as a banner and a highlighted row in Market.
- [ ] A banner slides down from the top; swipe up dismisses; urgent ones (payroll) stay until you act.
- [ ] Tap a banner: it opens the decision or asks "Go there"; Not now closes it.
- [ ] The Payment banner shows the largest real payment of the day.
- [ ] Popups: dim backdrop, Close works, VoiceOver reads the popup alone.
- [ ] Signing sheets: the "I confirm" box toggles; VoiceOver says Checked / Unchecked.

## Animations and accessibility
- [ ] Reduce Motion on: banners, popups, charts and section changes appear without sliding or drawing.
- [ ] Largest text size: small one-line labels shrink instead of cutting off.
- [ ] VoiceOver: stepper buttons say Decrease / Increase; also Remove this promise, Clear search.
- [ ] Launch: faces, car/logo/luxury/premises/trophy art, AirStay and TechPro photos, thumbnails and the app icon all render.

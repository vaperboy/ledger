## Current state in one paragraph
Five tabs (Home, Team, Group, Projects, Phone) already exist. Phone already holds Inbox, Bank, News, Reports; there is NO Accounting app and Market is a Group-tab page. Navigation has verified bugs; Company navigation is an underline row over 9+ sections (mockup: six-row hub). Hiring is a one-page offer with hidden ask, no working percentages, no promises, and an employer-cost preview that is wrong for part-time/seasonal. Several startup/investment/board mechanics are broken or hollow.

## Ownership map (one canonical home; other places link)
- **Home**: portfolio overview, top priorities (links to Inbox), company shortcuts.
- **Team**: people across businesses, hiring entry, hierarchy, vacancies, requests/promises overview. Company > People: that company's staff, contracts, promises, compact metrics.
- **Group**: ownership, holdings, chains, franchises, boards, group-level capital, stand-alone list, group office.
- **Projects**: ongoing/completed projects, milestones, tenders, ventures (Ventures home: owner question Q3).
- **Phone**: Inbox, Bank (accounts, transfers, loans, payment execution, personal finance), Accounting (transactions, payroll run and payslips, invoices, suppliers, documents, settings/access), Market (business selector), News, Reports, other utilities.
- **Company** (hub, six rows): Overview; People; Operations (premises, equipment, inventory, upkeep, suppliers purchasing, workplace, coverage); Sales & Market (pricing editor, marketing, how you sell, customers, market link); Stats & Information (cash-first figures, expense breakdown, legal/ownership, documents link); Projects.
- Single authorities: employer cost = one function; payment execution = Payments/Ledger via Bank; supplier records = Accounting; price = one editor writing the pricing source.

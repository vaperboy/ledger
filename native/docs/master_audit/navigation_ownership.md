# Stage 0 audit: navigation, feature ownership, Company operations (Stages 1 and 5)

Auditor: navigation/ownership. Base: `origin/native-swiftui` at dd580ef. Method: code reading only (no build, no run; the app builds only on macOS CI), plus `git log/diff` on remote branches. Anything not read in code is marked INFERENCE. Earlier audits (`docs/audit/ui-and-data.md` UI-01, UI-12, UI-26) were re-checked: UI-12 (More only on two tabs) is obsolete; UI-01 (side-scrolling tabs) is only partly fixed (N-11).

Row format: ID | Problem | Current behaviour (file:line) | Root cause | Expected (spec) | Proposed fix | Affected | Dependencies | Risk | Stage | Approval.
Paths are under `native/App/Ledger/` unless stated. Engine = `native/Engine/Sources/`.

## 0. Headline

* The five-tab shell the spec asks for ALREADY EXISTS: Home / Team / Group / Projects / Phone (`Core/GameStore.swift:15-27`, `Features/Root/RootView.swift:37-114`). The enum case for Home is still named `.company` (raw value used by deep links). The Inbox is already an app inside Phone, Bank/News/Reports too. Stage 1A is therefore NOT a restructure of the tab bar; it is (a) tap-selected-tab-to-root, (b) stale/duplicate route handling, (c) moving Market and (new) Accounting into Phone, (d) the Company section structure, (e) one-home-per-feature clean-up.
* There is NO Accounting app and NO Phone Market app today. Market is a Group-tab page (`CompanyRoute.market`) plus a per-company section.
* Company navigation today is `Overview / Team / Finances / Operations / Marketing / Customers / Market / Setup (+family sections)` as an underline row with a "More" menu (`Features/Companies/CompanyDetailView.swift:9-17, 93-120`). The mockup is a hub list of six rows (Overview, People, Operations, Sales & Market, Stats & Information, Projects). That is a real structural change, and it collides with owner decisions (section 11 CONFLICTS).
* Price is one slider (-3..+3) per company today. There is no per-product price, no price per hour, no percentage markup in the engine. The engine already has one number everything reads (`Company.priceLevel`), which gives a compatible way in (P-01).
* Chain/franchise: the chain registration (4 weeks) and franchise documents (6 weeks) have a real waiting period and a fee; the franchise BRAND application has a 1-3 week review and may be refused. Nothing is recorded in Accounting (no Accounting exists) and there is no sign-and-submit step (F-01).

## 1. Navigation today (verified)

**Root/tab structure.** `RootView` -> `GameShell` -> iPhone `PhoneShell` (TabView with system tab bar hidden, floating `PillTabBar`, `Features/Root/RootView.swift:37-63`) or iPad `SidebarShell` (NavigationSplitView, only the selected tab is mounted, `.id(store.tab)` at `RootView.swift:98`, so switching tabs discards that tab's stack on iPad only). Tab -> root view: `.company`=`CompaniesView` (shows "Home": `HomeSummary` + company list + `AdvanceBar`), `.team`=`TeamView`, `.group`=`GroupView`, `.projects`=`ProjectsView`, `.phone`=`PhoneHome` (`RootView.swift:103-114`). Each tab owns its own `NavigationStack` with a typed `[CompanyRoute]` path (Phone: `[PhoneHome.Page]`, `PhoneHome.swift:12-18`). `CompanyRoute` = `.company(id) | .companyAt(id, section) | .newCompany | .market` (`Features/Companies/CompaniesView.swift:6-10`), resolved in one place (`Features/Root/CompanyRouteDestinations.swift`).

**Tab names today vs required.** Home (case `.company`), Team, Group, Projects, Phone. Required: Home, Team, Group, Projects, Phone. The old Company/Inbox names are gone from the UI. Remaining stale names: doc comments (`GameStore.swift:11-14` still says "Company ... Inbox tab"), UI test helper `tab("Company")` in unmerged branch `test/automated-pass`.

**Phone today (13 apps, `Features/Phone/PhoneApps.swift:5-6`):** bank, inbox, news, weather, stats, goals, world, luxury, ventures, richList, reports, achievements, settings. There is no global "More" screen: "More" exists only as (1) the overflow menu of `SectionTabs` (Company sections) and (2) the Group tab's "More" menu of 10 pages (`GroupTabs.swift:11-38, 64-71`). Missing for the spec: Accounting, Market.

## 2. Company navigation vs the Company Management mockup

Mockup (`design/mockups/master/company_management.png`, read): Company hub = 6 rows (Overview "Key figures and alerts", People "Team and management", Operations "Premises, equipment, inventory", Sales & Market "Customers, pricing, marketing", Stats & Information "Performance, finances, documents", Projects "Ongoing projects and milestones"). Overview = Overview/Performance/Alerts tabs: Cash (big) + Profit/Margin, Key figures (sales, operating costs, employees), maintenance alert, Top actions. People = Team/Hierarchy tabs + 3 rows (Employees, Hiring, Management); Employees = search + list; Add manager = role/access/salary. Operations = Premises & Locations, Equipment, Inventory, Suppliers. Sales & Market = Sales & Market (products and services), Marketing, Customers, Market Rerrket [sic: "Market Pulse"/"Market Rerrket", demand, competition and trends]. Stats & Information = Performance/Financials/Documents tabs, "Where money goes" donut, Margin chart, Company information, Documents. Projects = Ongoing/Completed + Project detail (Overview/Milestones). Footer: "What moves to other apps": Bank (accounts, transfers, loans, financing, tax authority filings and payments), Accounting (bookkeeping, transactions, payroll, invoices, supplier records, contracts, accounting documents), Suppliers (supplier management and purchasing), Market (global market information, shared app).

Current `CompanySection` (`CompanyDetailView.swift:9-17`): overview, projects, staff("Team"), finances, operations, marketing, customers, market, setup; family-only: properties, book, energy("Plant"), salmon("Fish"), holding, club, chain. Primary row: Overview, Team, Finances, Operations + More (`CompanyDetailView.swift:99,117`). Marketing and Properties are folded into Operations by `redirect` (132-137).

## 6. Pricing (verified)

How price works now:
* One number per company: `Company.priceLevel` in -3...+3, half steps (`PlayerActions.swift:34-39` `setPriceLevel`, clamp -3,3). It maps to a multiplier by the table `[0.7,0.8,0.9,1,1.15,1.4,1.9]` (names Budget...Luxury, `Resources/balance.json priceLevels`, `Balance.priceMultiplier`, `Balance.swift:92-97`). The multiplier scales one ticket per company (`Pricing.ticket` = `bd.ticket * bd.ticketMultiplier * ticketMultiplier(...)`, `Pricing.swift:58-80`; unit per trade e.g. "billable hour", "basket", "order", "ride"; the ticket appears to be net of VAT because VAT is added at booking, `CompanyWeek.swift:102-122`: INFERENCE, not run). The UI words (Bargain...Luxury) and the numeric `%+.1f` are shown in the Operations "Price" row (`CompanyOperations.swift:144-145`).
* Everything price-sensitive reads `priceLevel`/`priceMultiplier`, not a money price: demand `Pricing.demandEffect` (97-140), reviews (`Reviews.swift:85`), satisfaction (`Satisfaction.swift:150`), competition (`Competition.swift:31`), customers' reference price `pref16` (`PriceMoves.weekly`), rental `1+0.08*level` (`RentalModel.swift:301`), finance premium (`FinanceModel.swift:115`), manager rules (`ManagerWeek.swift:91,172-175,359-362`), rival price war sets -3 (`Rivals.swift:525,544`, `RivalAbsorb.swift:227,247`), chain rule `rules.price` pushed to stores (`Chains.swift:107,274`).
* Effects of a change: the multiplier applies to the next weekly run (`CompanyWeek` ticket) and demand reacts with the elasticity curve; "hold my prices" (`inflHold`, `setInflationHold`) freezes the nominal price against inflation.
* Percent moves exist already: `PriceMoves.changePrice(byShare:)` (`PriceMoves.swift:~90-104`) is still stored as a `priceLevel` (via `Balance.priceLevel(multiplier:)`, clamped to level range, i.e. -30% to +90% around the market). Discount campaigns (`camp16`, `startDiscount`, depth limits per trade `PriceMoves.discountMax`), price war, and volume-contract bid price factor `[0.85...1.1]` (`CompanyContracts.swift:20`) are separate concepts.
* Editors today: (1) Operations "Price" sheet = the slider + hold prices (`CompanyOperationsSheets.swift:114-178`); (2) Company -> Market -> Pricing = % change, discount, price war (`CompanyMarket.swift:200-400`); (3) Chain page price rule (`ChainView.swift:335-345`); plus managers and rivals changing it automatically. No per-product price exists in the engine (one ticket per company; "product" variety is knobs/concepts/equipment), no price per hour as a typed amount, no percentage markup over cost (cost of sales exists as `cogs` share of ticket per trade: `trades.json`, e.g. grocery 0.75, boutique 0.52, cafe 0.30, tutor 0.03).

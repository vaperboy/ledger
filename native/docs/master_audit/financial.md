# Stage 0 audit: financial systems, banking, accounting, company conversion (Stage 2 area)

Scope: MASTER_SPEC Stage 2 (A to H). Mockups viewed: `banking_app.png`, `accounting_app.png`. Branch native-swiftui at the time of the audit.
Method: code read plus throwaway probe tests run on a copy of `native/Engine` outside the repo (Swift 6.0.3, Linux). Nothing in the repo was changed. The app (SwiftUI) was audited by reading only.
Probes run: sole-trader cafe (staff hired, 3M business loan, standard equipment/stock/documents, played 70 days) converted in UK, US, DE, NO, MX, then played 100 and 400 more days, save/load round trip, repeat conversion, insufficient funds. Also tax-estimate, savings, loan early-repay, net-worth and home-trade cost probes. Everything marked INFERENCE was not run.
Old audits (`native/docs/audit/*.md`) were reused only where re-verified: ledger gaps stay empty and equity reconciles (confirmed again: `Ledger.gaps` empty in every probe).

## 1. Company conversion (sole trader to limited company)

### 1.1 Trace
- UI: Company > Finances > Settings > "Legal form" card, `App/Ledger/Features/Companies/CompanySettings.swift:37-70` (`legalForm`, `confirmConvert`). Only entry point in the app.
- Cost read: `GameEngine.convertToLimitedCost`, `Engine/Sources/Simulation/CompanyScreenReads.swift:85` (country registration fee + minimum share capital; does not check company status).
- Action: `GameEngine.convertToLimited`, `Engine/Sources/Simulation/MiscActions.swift:192-210`. Whole body: refuse if already limited; refuse if `s.cash < fee + minimum capital`; `Payments.playerPays` fee and capital from the PERSONAL account; `Ledger.moveRaw` capital into company cash; set `shareCapital`, `injectedCapital += capital`, `ownerNetFlow += capital`; `form = .limited`; one company transaction "Share capital paid in"; one note.
- Everything else is driven by the single `form` flag read in about 60 places (Valuation, MonthEnd, PersonalFinance, Payments, Failure, Documents, CompanyCosts, Funding, Shareholders, HoldingModel...). `CompanySetup.create` (the new-limited-company path, `CompanySetup.swift:318-327`) sets more than `convertToLimited` does.

### 1.2 What changes and what does not (probed, 5 countries, same pattern in all)
Changes: `form`; `shareCapital`/`injectedCapital` (UK 13.54, NO 30,000, DE 290,761, US and MX about 0 in game units); company cash + capital; personal cash minus fee minus capital; sole-trader tax treatment stops, corporate tax accrual starts at the next month close; owner can now take dividends, set an owner salary, join a holding, raise funding; loans' liability rules unchanged (see C5); valuation multiplier `0.85 -> 1` (`Valuation.swift:52`).
Does NOT change: company id, name, ownership (100%, `holders` empty), staff and contracts (same employer id, correct as the entity continues), equipment, stock, loans, bank account (one cash balance per company), VAT registration, registration document, group membership (`holdingID`), books (year-to-date figures carry on), net worth (rises only by the capital moved in; falls by the fee).

## 2. Financial systems map (what exists today, for the one-workstream design)

### 2.1 Money model (one authority, keep it)
- Cash holders: `Ledger` (`Finance/Ledger.swift`): `.me` (personal current account, `GameState.cash`, statement of 600 lines, overdraft `overdraftLimit`) and `.company(id)` (`Company.cash`). Savings is `GameState.savings` (not a holder; fixed deposits in `rest["inv"]`). Every change goes through `Ledger.move/moveRaw`; `Ledger.gaps` proves cash equals booked flows.
- One account per company. No company sub-accounts (Payroll, Tax, Savings in the Banking mockup) and no company-to-company transfers except dividends/intercompany supply.
- Payment engine entry points (`Finance/Payments.swift`): `companyPays` (:19; category books the expense, label adds a transaction), `companyReceives` (:59), `book` (:73 expense without cash), `playerPays/Receives` (:99/:102), `transferFromSavings` (:108), `injectCash` (:122), `withdrawCash` (:136), `payDividend` (:158), `ownerReceivesSale` (:85); loan instalments `companyPaysLoanInstalment`/`playerPaysLoanInstalment`; `Banking.take/repay/close` (`Finance/Banking.swift`). Bills, payroll, tax, VAT: `Payday` (`Simulation/Payday.swift`), `MonthEnd.settleTax`, `SupplyTerms`, early payment `payObligationNow` (`Simulation/EarlyPayment.swift`). Reads: `BankReads.swift` (accounts, detail, transactions, statements), `FinanceReport.swift` (`financeOverview`, `Obligation` list), `PayrollReads.swift`.
- Records: `Company.books` (category totals week/month/year/lastYear/total: rev, cogs, wages, rent, mkt, ins, oth, dep, int, fine, loss, acc, tax), `Company.transactions` (`tx`, capped at 40, newest first, `Company.swift:131-137`), `monthlyHistory`, `payables`, `receivables`, `vatBalance`, `taxAccrued`, reports in `rest["reports"]` (week/month/year, `ReportModels.swift`).

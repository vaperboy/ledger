# Owner brief, 4 Nov 2026 (verbatim, binding): car dealerships and real products for every company

Used car dealership needs rework, same with other companies that are middle men. To have something to sell, you need to have the product to sell; currently it automatically just appears out of nowhere. The marketplace is where the used car dealership gets products to sell. It should be a realistic setup: you buy broken and/or used cars, use your own company (mechanic, used as supplier) to repair them so you can sell them for profit. Each car should show its own buy price, repair costs, and the same price-setting slider that shows live what would give profit and what would give loss. Some cars get stuck and sometimes you're forced to sell at a loss. Premises decide how many lot spaces you have. Fixing time also greys out the car on the sales page (your products), but people can still want to buy it and reserve it for when it's fixed. Tools that the company needs are only some cleaning products and a CRM system. Also profits for car sales show as 0 (sent screenshot: it always prompts negative numbers since it thinks it is going downwards). Staff required are salespeople, and a guy that buys the car (without one you have to manually buy them; a manager can do it too; how good deals he negotiated decides his skill).

Car dealership (new cars) should follow the same logic but more expensive to start up since it needs a showroom. A car dealership can also sign a brand contract: it gets one specific car brand to sell, and the dealership gets a realistic percentage of the sales. The car dealership chooses the "supplier" that supplies cars (not as big a sales margin as possible for used since more things are more automated). If a brand deal is signed it replaces the "generic" supplier with the brand only and nothing else can be picked until the contract ends, with possibility to renew if the brand is happy with the results.

For both car sales companies: dealer warranty, car loans through the bank (cost to set up and a realistic percentage for each sale). Also offer insurance: it should be possible, giving a realistic percentage as a one-time commission IF the customer buys it through you (high pick rate since it is cheaper than buying insurance regularly for customers).

Do a big audit of all companies: more companies don't just have a generic "your products" and should actually have, based on what companies do in real life, things they sell, because currently it has demand based on price and it feels like you're just selling titles, not real things. Each product and service should have the same tabs as employees do inside Team. Make sure you don't half-ass the job and do it PROPERLY for EVERY AND EACH company before you can say you're finished.

Both dealership jobs can have commission only, base+commission, and other salary types the job has.
